# Northwood

> Northwood (Northwood Space) builds satellite ground infrastructure: the Portal multi-beam phased-array antenna, the Prism high-data-rate parabolic antenna, the software that orchestrates every spacecraft contact, and a shared global ground network for commercial and government operators. El Segundo, California; founded 2022.
